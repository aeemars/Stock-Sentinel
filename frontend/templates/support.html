Frequently Asked Questions

How do the AI alerts work?

Alerts are generated daily at market close and can be triggered on-demand. Our OpenRouter AI intelligence engine analyzes holding metrics, market momentum, and entry prices to generate structured, actionable recommendations.

How do I add more portfolios?

Navigate to the Portfolios page and use the "Quick Create" widget on the left side to add new asset collections instantly.

How do I trigger an AI analysis scan?

You can click "Run AI Analysis" directly inside any portfolio or use the "Run AI Scan Now" button in the Alerts feed to evaluate your holdings at any time.
